## Goal

One coherent look for the whole game, which the Unity and Blender work will match:
stylised realism, clean silhouettes, readable colours, accurate proportions (design
section 15).

## Deliverables

1. `assets/media/style/STYLE_GUIDE.md` — palette with hex values (colour-blind safe;
   green / amber / red status colours that also work with icons, design section 14.4),
   typography direction, lighting and material notes, do and don't examples.
2. Style frames, 16:9 at 1920×1080 or larger, in `assets/media/style/frames/`:
   1. Title screen: Earth from orbit at the terminator, rocket silhouette, logo space.
   2. Build Bay: rocket on a launch-pad gantry at dusk, cranes, with empty regions left
      and right for dashboard and vote panels.
   3. Lift-off and ascent seen from a tracking camera.
   4. Orbit operations: satellite with solar wings over Earth.
   5. Reentry: capsule in plasma glow.
   6. Mission Control room with consoles and a big screen.
3. Key art for the project page and trailer thumbnail (16:9 and 1:1).
4. Mission briefing cinematic for contract C1 Earth Watch, 8 to 15 seconds, no text
   baked in, in `assets/media/cinematics/`.
5. `assets/media/CREDITS.md` — for every file: tool, model, prompt, date. This is required
   for the competition's AI-use disclosure.

## Rules

- No NASA logos, insignia or real mission patches. No real company liveries. No text or
  watermarks baked into images.
- Hardware must look plausible: correct engine-bell proportions, real staging layout.
- Files under 50 MB each; video as H.264 MP4.

## Acceptance

- All files exist at the paths above and open correctly; the six frames read as one game.
- `STYLE_GUIDE.md` is specific enough for a 3D artist to match materials and lighting.
- Evidence: a contact sheet image of all outputs in `orchestration/evidence/T005/`.

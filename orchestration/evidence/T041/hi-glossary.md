# Hindi (hi) translation glossary and rules — T041

Audience: students aged 12–18. Tone: respectful, friendly, clear; address the player as **आप** (never तू/तुम); imperatives in the polite **-इए / -ें** form (चुनिए / चुनें, दबाइए / दबाएँ, छूइए). Use one form throughout: **-ें** short imperatives (चुनें, दबाएँ, खींचें, छुएँ). Simple Hindi with common Devanagari science terms; avoid heavy Sanskritised words. English loan words that students really use may stay in Devanagari (e.g. सैटेलाइट or उपग्रह — use **उपग्रह**).

## Keep in Latin script (never translate or transliterate)
Role/console IDs (FLIGHT, BOOSTER, STRUCT, GUIDO, FIDO, EECOM, INCO, PAYLOAD, RETRO, PM), call-outs the game shows as codes (GO, NO-GO, ABORT), unit symbols (m/s, km, kN, kg, t, s, %, °, W, Wh, M$, $), part/mission/vehicle proper names (Falcon 9, Saturn V, Apollo 11, Starship, Soyuz, Electron, Ariane 5, Landsat, Mars Climate Orbiter, Deep Space Network, Kourou, Vandenberg), acronyms (LOX, LH₂, RCS, DSN, TWR, Isp, Δv, Max Q, PDR, CDR, SI, LEO, GEO, SSO, ISS, NASA, ESA, CNES, JPL), numeric IDs (F1…, C3, MG-01), keyboard keys (Space, Enter, S, E, I), code-ish tokens. Placeholders `{like_this}` must be copied exactly (same names, same count). Numbers stay in **Western digits (0–9)**, never Devanagari digits. Keep the `·`, `→`, `×`, `≥`, `±`, `Δ` characters. English text inside `.source` keys (source citations) stays English, copy it unchanged.

## Facts
Strings under `fact.*.text` must be **at most 25 words** in Hindi (count by spaces), accurate (no new numbers; do not change any number or date), and keep names in Latin script as above. Real tragedies are never jokes or "game over" gags.

## Punctuation
Use the Devanagari danda `।` to end sentences; keep `?`, `!`, `:`, `;` as in English with no preceding space. Keep `…`. Do not add Hindi digits.

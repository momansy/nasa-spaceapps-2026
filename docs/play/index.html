MISSIONFORGE

A team mission-design game

Build a spacecraft with your team: choose the parts, balance mass, power, budget, instruments, orbit and communications, then vote and launch together. One big screen shows the mission; everyone plays on their phone. Unofficial educational game. Not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.

How a session runs

  1. One screen hosts; up to ten players join with a room code.
  2. The crew votes on each part: its mass, power, cost and risk are shown for every option.
  3. Launch, fly and fix problems together; each role has its own console.
  4. See the score and debrief what the physics and the budget taught you.
